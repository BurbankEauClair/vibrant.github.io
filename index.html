What we do

Three ways we move a company.

Senior operators who have built, run and raised for companies themselves. No hand-offs to juniors, no decks without a product behind them.

Select & own

Ownership in a few companies we believe in.

We choose carefully and commit fully, with equity on the line and a seat at the table.

  • Equity and sweat-equity deals
  • Board and advisory seats
  • Co-founding new ventures
Allocate & lead

Resources where they move the valuation.

Interim leadership and build capacity, placed exactly where the company is stuck. Product is built by Rocket Factory.

  • Interim CEO, CTO and CPO
  • Product and build via Rocket Factory
  • Risk assessments and due diligence
Raise & scale

Investor-ready, then funded.

We sharpen the story, the numbers and the data room, then open our network and run the process with you.

  • Equity story and financial model
  • Data room and due diligence prep
  • Investor introductions and round management
